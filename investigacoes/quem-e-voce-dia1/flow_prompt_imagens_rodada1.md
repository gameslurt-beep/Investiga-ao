THE STORY WORLD:
A small, cozy, lived-in family house at night (one floor): entrance hall, a long corridor, living room, kitchen, and a bedroom with a desk and a tall wardrobe. Someone in a dark hooded jacket and black gloves walks through it with a phone flashlight. A cat lives there. A woman hides inside the bedroom wardrobe.

STYLE FOR ALL IMAGES:
Cinematic painterly illustration, like a high-end animated feature film: soft painted shapes, rich lighting, gentle film-like depth, subtle brush texture. Stylized, not photoreal. Night palette: everything in deep navy and blue-black shadows; the only warm colour is amber light (lamps in the LIT versions, the flashlight later). Faces are never shown clearly: characters are seen from behind, in silhouette, or with the face in shadow. NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO logos anywhere.
"Green background" means: PURE FLAT CHROMA GREEN (#00FF00), evenly lit, no floor, no ground shadow, no other objects, items spaced apart and not touching.
"LIT version" means: the room softly lit by warm lamps, every detail readable.
"DARK version" means: EXACTLY the same image, same framing, same objects in the same places, but with all lamps off; only faint cold blue moonlight from windows, most details barely visible.
